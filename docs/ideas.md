# System Ideas & Improvement Backlog

Ideas for future development of the system itself (skills, automations, surfaces), not life tasks. No priority order: a place to capture them before they become a task or a project.

---

## How to add one

Add a `###` heading under the category that fits (or a new `##` category), then two or three sentences: what it would do and why it would help. When you decide to build one, turn it into a task with `/pfc-add-task` and delete it here.

---

## Integrations

### Example: voice capture
Talk to the agent hands-free: speech-to-text input runs the same skills (task capture, habit logs, rating the day), with replies spoken back. Useful while getting ready or driving.
